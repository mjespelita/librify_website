About Us

Librify Information Technology Solutions is a reputable local internet provider in Northern Samar, dedicated to advancing the region's communication infrastructure. In addition to improving connectivity, Librify is deeply committed to fostering opportunities through community engagement and initiatives. These efforts are aimed at contributing to the overall development and progress of the community, reflecting the company's mission to support both technological and social advancement in Northern Samar.

Librify offers a complete range of fiber internet solutions/services such as high-speed internet, unlimited data, affordable price for monthly plans, reliable service that comes with a 24Hrs online customer service/helpdesk support together with its new and upgraded version of free Librify TV Application. Providing Free internet connections to schools, Barangay’s, public parks and municipal halls here in Northern Samar is just one of the Librify’s integration into government projects which helps enhance community access to Information and Technology. This year 2025, Librify’s latest upgrade/innovation is utilizing the Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD). This approach represents a significant advancement in ensuring a more fast, reliable, secure and protected internet service. It also fosters digital inclusion and service reliability as it will reduce the risk of damage to fiber lines, ensures safer installations, and will minimize potential internet disruptions.

Looking forward, Librify is set to offer its customers fiber internet connectivity that is extremely affordable for both personal and business use. This will allow the subscriber to access services like gaming, e- commerce, and video on demand at a very reasonable cost under secure conditional access and advanced digital experience.

Our Support Team

1. Why is my internet connection slow?

Slow speeds can be caused by network congestion, outdated equipment, or interference. Try restarting your router or contacting support for help.

2. How do I apply for a new connection?

You can apply through our website, visit our office, or call our customer service for assistance.

3. When will my installation be completed?

Installation usually takes 1–3 business days after your application is approved. We’ll contact you to confirm the exact date.

4. How can I pay my bill?

Payments can be made online, via mobile app, through bank transfer, or in person at our office.
